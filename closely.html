Solo SDRs or small teams whose primary channel is LinkedIn outreach combined with email should consider Closely for its unified campaign builder and low entry price. Teams that send high daily volume or need predictable costs without credit caps should skip it and choose Instantly or HeyReach instead. The tool works until AI credits run out or LinkedIn flags appear.
Strengths
  • Unified LinkedIn + email sequences in one campaign builder for solo users who otherwise switch between tools.
  • AI personalization that produces usable first-touch copy when you supply a good prompt and still have credits.
  • Safety limits and delays that let some users run campaigns for months without LinkedIn restrictions.
    • Limitations
  • AI credits on the Growth plan are consumed in 2-3 weeks of daily multi-channel use, forcing most teams to Enterprise pricing.
  • LinkedIn account flags remain a real risk even with safety settings, requiring manual intervention when sequences break.
  • Occasional bugs and the need to repeatedly tweak prompts add friction that interrupts automated outreach.

    Typical alternatives 04
    Instantly
    Instantly focuses on cold email at scale with unlimited sending and strong deliverability tools, while Closely centers on unified LinkedIn + email sequences with built-in lead database and AI personalization credits.
    Choose Instantly when your volume is primarily cold email and you want predictable monthly costs without credit caps. Choose Closely when LinkedIn outreach is your primary channel and you need one platform for both channels.
    HeyReach
    HeyReach offers multi-channel outreach with LinkedIn, email, and Twitter plus more advanced analytics and team features, whereas Closely is simpler, cheaper for solo users, but has basic reporting and stricter AI credit limits.
    Choose HeyReach when you run team campaigns or need deeper analytics and are willing to pay more. Choose Closely when you are a solo SDR or small team focused only on LinkedIn + email and want lower upfront cost.
    Inside the workflow 05
    You log into Closely, connect your LinkedIn and email accounts, then build a campaign by uploading or scraping a lead list into the contact database. You write a prompt for AI personalization, select multi-channel sequences mixing LinkedIn messages and emails, set safety limits, and launch. Each morning you check the dashboard for replies, approve or edit AI-generated follow-ups in the inbox, and monitor analytics for acceptance and response rates.
