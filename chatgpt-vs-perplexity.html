ChatGPT vs Perplexity: which should you use?

Side-by-side comparison based on real user signals, verified pricing, and honest verdicts.

Updated 2026-04-26 · By Riley Voss
When to choose which

Choose Perplexity when you need real-time accurate research with citations and minimal hallucination risk. Choose ChatGPT when you need a versatile assistant for product vision briefs, roadmap drafting, coding, and iterative creative work across multiple modalities.

Key differences

Perplexity pulls live web data for every query and always cites sources, while ChatGPT relies on a static cutoff unless you enable browsing and still frequently hallucinates facts. Perplexity responses are concise and research-focused; ChatGPT is more versatile for writing roadmaps, generating code, or creative brainstorming.

Perplexity pulls live web data with inline citations on every response while ChatGPT relies on a static cutoff unless you enable browsing in Plus; Perplexity returns concise cited answers whereas ChatGPT produces longer, more flexible creative or conversational output.
