Shopify brand managers or agencies who already exhaust their time building segment-specific landing pages should consider the $199/month Developer plan only after they have validated conversion lift on a few manual pages. Everyone else should skip it: the Free plan ends after 2-3 generations, there are zero public reviews confirming results, and the output still needs significant manual editing.

  • One-click publish and built-in analytics keep the entire loop inside the Shopify ecosystem for simple campaigns.
  • Unlimited generations after the $199/month upgrade removes the quota friction that stops most users on Free.
    • Limitations
  • Free plan quota ends after 2-3 campaigns, forcing an immediate jump to the $199/month Developer tier with no mid-tier option.

    Inside the workflow 05
    You log into the Auware dashboard, connect your Shopify store once, then describe a target customer segment in plain English. You click Generate to trigger automated audience-specific landing page creation, review the output in the preview pane, push it live to Shopify with one click, and track performance metrics inside the built-in analytics tab. For the next campaign you repeat the process, but after 2-3 generations the Free plan quota ends and you must upgrade to Developer to continue.
    • —Free plan quota is exhausted after only 2-3 campaigns per month, forcing an immediate $199/month jump with no mid-tier option.
    • —Zero public reviews or community signals make it impossible to know whether the generated pages actually convert before you pay.
    • —Full Shopify integration works only after the Developer upgrade, so early testing requires committing real budget or staying inside the limited trial.
