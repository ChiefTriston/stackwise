Screenshot of Otter.ai — captured from official site
Sales and account executives who run frequent 1:1 or 2-person discovery and pipeline calls with clear American English should use Otter for its searchable transcripts and action-item summaries. Teams with heavy accent diversity, noisy environments, or more than two speakers should skip it because speaker ID and accuracy issues erase most time savings and force constant manual corrections.
Strengths
  • Delivers real-time transcripts with live captions and searchable archives for clean 1:1 or 2-person American-English meetings, replacing manual note-taking and email follow-ups.
  • Generates usable meeting summaries and action items that integrate with calendar and CRM exports when the audio is high-quality.
  • Supports live team commenting on the transcript during the call for collaborative review without separate note docs.
    • Limitations
  • Pro's 5000-minute monthly limit is consumed at 1:1 with meeting time, forcing most 10+ hour weekly users onto the $20/user Business plan within the first quarter.
  • Speaker identification frequently fails with three or more participants, requiring manual corrections that can exceed the claimed time savings.
  • Accuracy drops sharply with non-American accents or background noise, turning the post-meeting edit loop into extra work.

    Best fit / not ideal for 03
    Best fit
    Sales and account executives running frequent 1:1 or 2-person discovery calls with clear American English who benefit from searchable historical transcripts and automated action items.
    Individual contributors who upload pre-recorded calls and need keyword search across past meetings without complex integrations.
    Teams already on Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams that value live captions and real-time commenting during meetings.
    Not ideal for
    Teams with diverse accents or background noise because transcription and speaker ID errors require more editing time than they save.
    Organizations needing on-device processing or unlimited minutes without cloud upload because Otter relies on cloud minutes that cap at the Pro tier.
    Project teams that require structured agendas, pre-meeting briefs, or deep CRM handoff because Otter focuses on transcription rather than meeting workflow orchestration.
    Typical alternatives 04
    Fellow
    Fellow bakes collaborative agendas, pre-meeting briefs, and 50+ native integrations (Slack, Asana, Hubspot) into every meeting workflow, while Otter focuses on real-time transcription and post-meeting search. Fellow stores all recordings and notes in one secure workspace with automatic post-meeting emails; Otter relies on its separate dashboard and requires manual exports.
    Choose Fellow when your team runs structured project or sales meetings that need agenda collaboration and CRM handoff. Choose Otter when you primarily need accurate real-time transcription and searchable historical meeting archives.
    Wave
    Wave runs on-device via Whisper v3, offers unlimited recording length and Siri integration on iOS with no monthly minute caps, while Otter depends on cloud processing with strict Pro/Business minute limits. Wave produces summaries from any audio (lectures, calls, appointments) but lacks live captions, speaker ID during meetings, and team workspaces.
    Choose Wave when you record solo or small-group audio on mobile and want zero minute limits or privacy from cloud uploads. Choose Otter when you need live meeting participation, multi-user collaboration, and enterprise admin controls.
    Inside the workflow 05
    You join a Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams call with Otter Assistant enabled, or you upload a pre-recorded file via the web or mobile app. The real-time transcript appears with live captions, speaker labels, and synced audio; you and teammates add comments or edit speaker names directly in the transcript pane while the meeting runs. After the call, you open the Otter dashboard, review the AI-generated summary and action items, correct any mislabeled speakers or mangled accents, then search past meetings by keyword or export the notes to your calendar and CRM.
