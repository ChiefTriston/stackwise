Screenshot of Proactor — captured from official site
Sales and account executives who run back-to-back calls and want an always-listening AI teammate that surfaces suggestions and drafts CRM tasks in the moment should use Proactor. Individual contributors who only need searchable transcripts or post-meeting polished notes should skip it and choose Otter.ai or Supernormal instead. Power users will hit the Pro tier's 20-25 hour monthly transcription limit quickly and face iOS performance hiccups that interrupt the real-time flow.
Strengths
  • Generates Smart Suggestions, action items, and CRM task drafts proactively during the live conversation when the app has sufficient permissions and credits remaining.
  • Delivers speaker-separated structured notes and AI Advice immediately after the meeting for sales follow-ups and client-call analysis.
  • Supports real-time transcription across mobile, iPad, and web with multi-language handling for global teams.
    • Limitations
  • Pro tier credits run out after 20-25 hours of monthly meeting transcription for daily users, forcing data-pack purchases or upgrade to Business.
  • iOS users encounter performance and speed problems that interrupt real-time transcription flow.
  • Constant background listening drains battery and raises privacy concerns for users who do not want an always-on AI teammate.

    Best fit / not ideal for 03
    Best fit
    Sales professionals who run frequent client calls and benefit from proactive suggestions and CRM task creation while the conversation is still live.
    Account managers who need structured notes, action items, and AI-generated follow-up drafts pushed directly into their CRM after each meeting.
    Students and interviewers who record lectures or candidate conversations and want speaker-separated summaries plus key-insight extraction without manual prompting.
    Not ideal for
    Users who need reliable, searchable transcripts on a tight budget and prefer to prompt the AI themselves after the meeting.
    Teams that record meetings asynchronously and prioritize clean, branded post-meeting notes over live suggestions.
    Note-takers who prefer to type their own points and only want lightweight AI cleanup rather than an always-listening proactive agent.
    Typical alternatives 04
    Otter.ai
    Otter.ai offers speaker identification and searchable transcripts but requires explicit commands for summaries and action items, whereas Proactor generates them proactively in real time without prompting. Otter's free tier gives 300 minutes per month while Proactor's free tier caps at 60 minutes plus limited AI credits.
    Choose Otter.ai when you need reliable, searchable transcripts on a budget and are comfortable prompting the AI yourself. Choose Proactor when you want an autonomous agent that anticipates tasks and drafts CRM entries during the conversation.
    Supernormal
    Supernormal focuses on post-meeting polished notes and templates with higher per-month transcription limits on its Pro plan, but it does not offer real-time transcription or proactive suggestions while you are still speaking. Supernormal's Business tier is priced similarly to Proactor's but lacks the live Smart Suggestions feature.
    Choose Supernormal when you record meetings asynchronously and prioritize clean, branded meeting notes after the fact. Choose Proactor when you need live insights, action items, and CRM updates while the conversation is still happening.
    Granola
    Granola is a lightweight notepad that uses AI to enhance what you type rather than transcribing audio; it has no real-time transcription or proactive agent behavior. Its pricing tiers are lower than Proactor's but it cannot replace an always-listening meeting copilot.
    Choose Granola when you prefer to take your own notes and just want AI cleanup. Choose Proactor when you want to stay fully present in the meeting while an AI teammate listens, summarizes, and acts autonomously.
    Inside the workflow 05
    You join a meeting on Zoom, Teams or in-person, open the Proactor mobile or web app, and hit Record. As people speak, real-time transcription appears in the live view while the proactive AI teammate silently listens in the background, inserting Smart Suggestions, background facts, and next-step prompts without being asked. After the meeting you review the automatically generated structured notes, speaker-separated summaries, action items, and AI Advice; you edit, export, or let it push tasks straight into your CRM before the next call starts.
    • —Pro credits run out after 20-25 hours of monthly meeting transcription for power users, forcing most individuals to buy data packs or upgrade to Business within the first month of consistent daily use.
    • —iOS users frequently hit performance and speed problems that interrupt real-time transcription flow, even though the developer is actively investigating.
    • —The proactive suggestions require constant background listening which drains battery and raises privacy concerns for anyone who does not want an always-on AI teammate.
