L&D teams and corporate training managers who produce ongoing libraries of localized, interactive compliance or onboarding videos should use Synthesia because it collapses script-to-video with built-in translation, quizzes, and team review. Solo creators or marketing teams needing photorealistic talking-head clips from a single photo should skip it and choose HeyGen instead. Heavy users on the Pro tier will hit the minute limit mid-month and face the steep jump to custom Enterprise pricing.
Strengths
  • Generates structured training videos with interactive quizzes and hotspots directly from scripts, replacing separate recording and editing steps for internal L&D workflows.
  • Delivers 1-Click Translation to 130+ languages with separate audio tracks while preserving on-screen elements, useful for global compliance programs but still requires final timing review.
  • Provides team workspaces, timestamped commenting, and brand kits on Pro and above, streamlining review cycles for corporate training teams that already work inside LMS environments.
    • Limitations
  • Avatar lip sync and facial expressions remain noticeably unnatural, forcing extra script revisions or reshooting before wide internal distribution.
  • Pro plan's 120-minute monthly credit is consumed at 1:1 ratio and exhausts quickly for teams producing more than four videos per user per week.
  • Avatar customization is limited to stock options until you reach Enterprise, creating brand-consistency friction for teams that need specific appearances.

    Best fit / not ideal for 03
    Best fit
    Corporate L&D managers building multilingual compliance or onboarding libraries who benefit from script-to-video speed plus native interactive elements and LMS export.
    Global training teams that already localize content across 10+ languages and can leverage the auto-translate and voice cloning features within the monthly credit limits.
    Enterprise compliance programs that require SSO, on-premise options, custom AI models, and dedicated support to meet security and audit standards.
    Not ideal for
    Marketing teams creating customer-facing promotional videos who need photorealistic avatars cloned from a single photo and will be slowed by Synthesia's template-heavy workflow.
    Solo creators or small businesses producing fewer than four videos per month who find the Pro price expensive relative to output and the robotic artifacts too noticeable for external use.
    Developers or product teams needing rapid screen recordings with branching logic who will hit the minute caps and limited avatar options before their volume justifies Enterprise.
    Typical alternatives 04
    HeyGen
    HeyGen offers stronger one-click translation and talking-photo features while Synthesia provides deeper enterprise SSO, on-premise options, and more polished stock avatars. HeyGen's interface emphasizes instant avatar cloning from a single photo; Synthesia's centers on script-to-video with 250+ templates and interactive video branching.
    Choose HeyGen when you need fast multilingual dubs and photo-based avatars for marketing content. Choose Synthesia when you require enterprise compliance, team workspaces, and interactive quizzes inside training videos.
    Colossyan
    Colossyan generates videos directly from text with fewer templates than Synthesia's 250+ library and lacks Synthesia's native interactive elements like hotspots and branching paths. Colossyan focuses on rapid text-to-video for simple explainers; Synthesia adds screen recording, voice cloning on Pro, and real-time team commenting.
    Choose Colossyan when you want the cheapest text-to-video option for one-off internal explainers under 10 minutes total. Choose Synthesia when you produce ongoing training libraries that need localization, interactivity, and brand kits.
    Inside the workflow 05
    You open the Synthesia dashboard, type a prompt or paste a script into the AI Video Assistant, select an avatar from the 150+ library and a template from the 250+ layouts, then click Generate. You review the draft in the AI Video Editor, tweak timing or add interactive elements like quizzes and hotspots, run 1-Click Translation for 130+ languages if needed, then share the link for timestamped team comments before exporting the MP4.

    Overview 07

    You spend hours in front of a camera or waiting on editors and voice talent to produce short training videos for onboarding, compliance, or product education. Synthesia removes that cycle by turning a script or prompt directly into a narrated video with an avatar, on-screen text, and interactive elements. The daily experience starts in the dashboard where you paste your script or use the AI Video Assistant, pick one of 150+ avatars and a template from the 250+ library, generate the draft, then edit timing, add quizzes or hotspots, run 1-Click Translation to 130+ languages, gather timestamped feedback, and export. Teams that create structured internal training in multiple languages benefit most, but they accept that avatar lip sync and facial expressions still look robotic to many viewers and that the Pro plan's 120-minute monthly credit is consumed at a strict 1:1 ratio, often forcing an Enterprise upgrade once volume exceeds four videos per user per week.
