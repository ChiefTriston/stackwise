    • Pricing 01

    GetResponse offers multiple pricing plans with two billing periods (monthly or 12 months). The Enterprise plan includes an additional 24-month billing option. A 14-day free trial is available with access to premium features but with limitations: webinars limited to 10 people, GetResponse badge on messages, landing pages limited to 1,000 unique visitors per month, and limited marketing automation features. The AI course creator allows unlimited courses for up to 250 students but selling requires a paid plan. Premium newsletters can be created with free subscriptions but paid subscriptions require a paid plan. The Enterprise plan offers a 30-day Pilot Program for testing. Customer support is available via live chat in English and Polish during specified hours, with 24/7 chatbot and email support in multiple languages. Specific pricing amounts are not provided in the source content.

    Typical alternatives 04
    ActiveCampaign
    ActiveCampaign ranks highest for overall flexibility and sophistication in handling complex automations, offering advanced visual builders with extensive conditional logic and AI features. However, its complexity can overwhelm beginners compared to GetResponse's more accessible drag-and-drop interface.
    Choose ActiveCampaign when you need sophisticated B2B automation with complex multi-step sequences and advanced conditional logic. Choose GetResponse when you want accessible automation tools with webinar integration and simpler workflow building.
    HubSpot
    HubSpot integrates automation tightly with its CRM for comprehensive lead scoring and deal-based workflows across channels. However, full omnichannel and advanced automation capabilities require Professional plans starting around $890/month, limiting depth in complex branching compared to GetResponse.
    Choose HubSpot when you need deep CRM integration with sales-driven workflows and have budget for higher-tier plans. Choose GetResponse when you want comprehensive automation features at more accessible pricing with webinar capabilities.
    Brevo
    Brevo offers an all-in-one marketing platform with email, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat in a single tool, particularly popular in Europe for GDPR compliance. It provides a robust free tier making it attractive for startups and SMBs seeking affordable alternatives.
    Choose Brevo when you need multi-channel communication (SMS, WhatsApp, chat) with GDPR compliance and want a strong free tier. Choose GetResponse when you prioritize email marketing automation with webinar integration and visual workflow builders.

    Overview 07

    GetResponse is an AI-powered email marketing and automation platform that helps businesses convert prospects into customers and grow repeat revenue through personalized communication. The platform addresses the challenge of turning 'maybe-later' prospects into 'yes-now' customers by providing automated email sequences, SMS marketing, landing pages, and sales funnels that engage audiences at optimal times throughout the customer journey. It solves problems related to cart abandonment, customer retention, lead nurturing, and scaling personalized marketing efforts without requiring extensive technical expertise or large marketing teams.

    The platform works by leveraging AI to tailor email content, recommend optimal send times, and automate customer journeys based on individual behavior patterns. Users can create automated workflows for welcoming new customers, recovering abandoned carts, and re-engaging inactive subscribers, while the AI determines the best timing for each interaction. GetResponse integrates with other business tools to maintain data synchronization and includes features like landing page creation with AI-generated copy, popup and banner management, webinar hosting, and SMS messaging - all designed to work together within automated sales funnels that guide prospects through the conversion process.

    GetResponse benefits most entrepreneurs, ecommerce stores, and growing businesses that need affordable, scalable marketing automation without complex setup requirements. The platform is particularly valuable for companies looking to maintain personalized customer communication as they scale, with pricing that remains accessible as businesses grow. Key tradeoffs include the platform's focus on email and automation marketing rather than being a comprehensive CRM solution, and while the AI features simplify many tasks, businesses with highly specialized or complex marketing needs might require more customizable solutions. The 5-star rated customer success support and no credit card required trial make it accessible for businesses testing marketing automation for the first time.
