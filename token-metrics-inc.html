    Typical alternatives 04
    CoinGecko
    CoinGecko provides free on-chain metrics, sentiment, and rankings without any AI wrapper or subscription, while Token Metrics charges $49.99–$99.99/month for its AI scores that users often say repackage the same public data.
    Choose CoinGecko when you want free, transparent data and are comfortable doing your own synthesis. Choose Token Metrics when you value the pre-ranked AI list and are willing to pay for the convenience and Telegram community.
    DexScreener
    DexScreener delivers real-time token launches, liquidity, and chart data for free with zero subscription, whereas Token Metrics bundles that information into paid monthly plans and adds AI ratings that critics call overhyped.
    Choose DexScreener when hunting new launches and on-chain momentum with no budget. Choose Token Metrics when you prefer curated daily rankings and educational reports over raw real-time charts.
