    Wordtune focuses on real-time paraphrasing, grammar checking, and clarity suggestions inside Google Docs or its editor with an Advanced plan at $6.99/month, whereas AISEO centers on bulk humanizing of AI drafts, built-in SEO scoring, and full article generation.
    Choose Wordtune when you edit existing human-written drafts sentence-by-sentence for tone and clarity. Choose AISEO when you start from AI-generated marketing or blog content that must pass detectors and rank in search.
    Inside the workflow 05
    You open the AISEO dashboard, paste AI-generated text into the Humanizer tool or upload a document, select target tone and one of 25+ languages, then click Humanize. You review the restructured output in the side-by-side editor, run the built-in AI detector score, and if needed iterate by adjusting readability sliders or rephrasing specific sentences. For full blog posts you switch to Article Writer mode, input a keyword brief, generate a draft, then immediately feed it back into the Humanizer before publishing.

    Overview 07

    You generate first drafts with AI tools then lose hours rewriting them to sound natural, pass detectors, and retain SEO keywords for blog posts or marketing pages. AISEO solves this exact loop by letting you paste the draft into the Humanizer, select tone and language, and receive restructured output that reduces mechanical patterns while preserving meaning and intent. You review the side-by-side editor, check the built-in detector score, tweak readability sliders if needed, and feed full articles from Article Writer mode straight back into the same flow before publishing.

    The daily experience starts in the dashboard or Chrome extension. You avoid copy-paste cycles, run the humanizer on AI-generated text, iterate once or twice on rhythm and phrasing, then move to SEO tools for keyword clustering or content briefs when scaling beyond one-off posts. The interface stays simple so the focus remains on output rather than learning curves.

    Solo writers and marketers who publish multiple SEO-driven pieces per week benefit most because the bundled Article Writer, humanizer, and on-page tools collapse several steps. The key tradeoff they accept is that detector bypass remains inconsistent, often requiring a final manual polish, and customer support is effectively unavailable when the output still gets flagged.
